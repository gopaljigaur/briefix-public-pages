Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

Welcome to Briefix (“we,” “our,” or “us”).
Briefix is a news aggregation application that collects publicly available news content from various sources and presents it in a short and easy-to-read format.

This Privacy Policy explains how Briefix handles information when you use our mobile applications on Android and iOS.

1. Information We Collect

No account registration or login is required to read news. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.

Your topic, language, appearance, and notification preferences, recently seen article IDs, and cached articles are stored on your device. Topic and language selections are used in requests for matching news. These preferences are not saved as a named user profile in our backend.

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications. Firebase processes app installation identifiers, messaging registration information, and device/app configuration such as device model, language, time zone, OS version, and app version to support message delivery. Firebase initialization can occur when the app starts, even if you have not enabled notification alerts.

Firebase and its dependencies also process technical diagnostic data and app/device information for service operation and SDK analytics. The included SDK privacy manifests describe these data types as not linked to your identity and not used for tracking.

Network requests to our service providers and news publishers also expose ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to those services.

We do not request access to your precise location, contacts, microphone, or camera. Notification alerts are optional and can require your device's notification permission. You can turn news notifications off in the app's Settings or manage notification permission in your device settings. Turning alerts off does not itself delete Firebase installation information.

If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and the information you include so that we can respond.

2. News Content

Briefix fetches and displays news content stored in a backend database powered by Supabase.

The app acts as a news aggregator and displays images and links from third-party news sources. Image hosts and websites you open have their own privacy policies and practices.

3. Analytics and Tracking

Briefix does not include advertising, app analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. The messaging identifiers described above support notifications and are not used by Briefix for advertising.

4. Advertising

Briefix currently does not display advertisements.

However, we may introduce third-party advertising services in future versions of the app. If advertising is added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe:

What data may be collected
Which advertising providers are used
How users can manage advertising preferences
5. Children’s Privacy

Briefix is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

Connections to Supabase and Firebase use HTTPS. Information stored on your device is handled by the app and your device's operating system.

7. Third-Party Services

Briefix uses Supabase to store and serve news articles, and Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging to support notifications. We do not maintain a list of individual device messaging tokens in the Briefix Supabase database; the app subscribes to a shared news topic.

You can read more about these providers' practices here:

Supabase Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Users may also interact with external news publisher websites through links displayed inside the app.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app or through an updated policy URL.
