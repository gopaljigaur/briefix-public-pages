We typically respond within 1–2 business days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I change my news topics?**
Go to the Settings tab (bottom right) and tap "Manage Topics" to add or remove your interests.

**The app isn't loading news. What should I check?**
Ensure you have an active internet connection. Pull down on the feed to refresh. If the issue persists, close and reopen the app.

**How do I turn off notifications?**
Go to the Settings tab and toggle off "Push Notifications."

**How do I report a problem with an article?**
Email us at contact@gopalji.me with the article title and a brief description of the issue.
